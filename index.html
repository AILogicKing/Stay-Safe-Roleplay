The safety sequence

Three actions. One clear priority: create distance and get help.

01

Get away

Move away from danger if you can do so safely. Leave belongings behind.

02

Get hidden

If leaving is unsafe, secure yourself out of view and silence your phone.

03

Get help

When safe, contact emergency responders and share your location.
